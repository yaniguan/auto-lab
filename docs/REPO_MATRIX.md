## `L6` · [lab-ask-why](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-ask-why) (`6f4887a`)

- **Tagline**: Socratic co-scientist combining two-stage hybrid RRF + 26-feature cross-feature reranking, 90% split-conformal 3-way stance verification, and structured control-well synthesis across 8 real corpora.
- **Object**: Epistemic disagreement and rationale-probing module (`L6`) for the Auto-Lab brain: rather than collapsing scientific uncertainty into a binary verdict, it retrieves verbatim evidence sentences on each side of a claim, surfaces necessary assumptions, and frames the discriminating experimental result that would resolve the disagreement.
- **Data**: Evaluates on SHA-256-verified SciFact (`809` train claims split `607` fit / `202` cal, `300` held-out dev claims with `0` claim-ID overlap, `209` evidence pairs across `5,183` abstracts; CC BY-NC 2.0, gitignored `data/`), an 8-corpus real downloaded benchmark (`results/open_benchmarks_2026.json`: `SciFact`, `PubMedQA`, `ConflictBank`, `FaithEval-Inconsistent`, `CLAIMCHECK`, `SciVer`, `ChemBench`, `LitQA2`), and a 14-dataset `OpenDatasetSpec` registry (`src/lab_ask_why/datasets.py`) with strict `eval_only` canary/training guards.
- **Model**: Zero-parameter biomedical Okapi `BM25` baseline plus a 2-stage `FrontierHybridVerifier` (`Reciprocal Rank Fusion` of `BM25` + 96-dim subword TF-IDF Truncated-SVD `LSA` + `EntityNumericNegationIndex`, followed by a 26-feature `CrossFeatureReranker` and `SplitConformalStanceClassifier` over `{SUPPORT, CONTRADICT, NEI}`), paired with Socratic `why_questions` and falsifiable `synthesize_control_wells`.
- **Training**: Trained strictly on `SciFact` `train_fit` (`607` claims) and calibrated on `train_calib` (`202` claims) with eval-abstract IDF exclusion (`93` dev docs excluded). Lifts held-out `SciFact` dev oracle rationale `hit@3` from `0.818` (`BM25`) and `0.528` (20-seed random) to `0.909` (`rationale_set_recall@3 = 0.876`, `hit@1 = 0.660`), achieves `0.907` empirical 90% split-conformal stance coverage, and reaches `100.0%` conflict detection on `ConflictBank` (`96.7%` on `FaithEval-Inconsistent`).
- **History**: Initialized in `6236123` (2026-11-18: SciFact `BM25` probe) -> `40af81d` (Phase-2 Lean 4 & `autolab-wire/v1`) -> `b8a9d51` (PR #31: 14-dataset 2024-2026 registry) -> `6f4887a` (PR #32: 2-stage hybrid reranker, split-conformal stance verifier, 8-corpus real benchmark, control-well synthesis, and Lean 4.36.0 parity CI).
- **Unique Properties**:
  - Two-stage Hybrid RRF + 26-feature `CrossFeatureReranker` lifting held-out SciFact dev `hit@3` to `0.909` (`0.876` full rationale-set recall@3) with zero LLM API calls.
  - Split-conformal 3-way stance sets (`{SUPPORT, CONTRADICT, NEI}`, `90.7%` empirical dev coverage at `alpha = 0.10`) detecting `100.0%` of conflicting `ConflictBank` contexts vs `0.0%` forced-binary collapse.
  - Fail-closed `autolab_wire.py` (`L6_MALFORMED_NEGATIVE_CERTIFICATE`) ingesting 64-hex SHA-256 `CERTIFIED_NEGATIVE` certificates from `lab-attrib` and synthesizing vehicle/precursor control wells.
  - Lean 4.36.0 `LabAskWhyLean` formal floor-permille & multi-claim consistency proofs verified in CI alongside strict `data_source` (`real_downloaded` vs opt-in `synthetic_fixture`) provenance.
- **Deep Profile**: [`lab-ask-why/docs/ARCHITECTURE_AND_HISTORY.md`](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-ask-why/blob/main/docs/ARCHITECTURE_AND_HISTORY.md)

- **Object**: Epistemic decision and applicability-domain layer (`L6`) under the Auto-Lab brain: delivers distribution-free calibrated prediction intervals inside its trusted chemical/experimental manifold, emits explicit 'I don't know' abstentions outside it, and recalibrates as new laboratory measurements expand the boundary.

- **Model**: Huber `GradientBoostingRegressor` / `LightGBM` surrogates paired with finite-sample Mondrian Split Conformal and locally scaled `cqr_r` (`LabDecideLean.conformalRank`, 23 Lean 4.36.0 theorems with `#print axioms` CI), `onehot_morgan` HTE encodings + train-only sub-bagged `ensemble_mahalanobis` kNN gating, deterministic balanced group-packing splits (`<= 0.67` pp error), and multi-round sequential BO (`tr_conformal_ucb`, `lightgbm_cqr_tr_ucb`).
- **Training**: Across 20 ESOL seeds, selective Mondrian conformal achieves `0.924 +- 0.028` inside coverage (`MAE_in 0.581 +- 0.040` vs `MAE_out 1.002 +- 0.161`; `seed=0` `0.936` inside cov, `MAE_in 0.640` vs `1.217`). Across all 11 real datasets, `11/11` achieve `gate_informative = True` (`5/5` seeds `MAE_in < MAE_out`). In 10-seed x 6-round BO campaigns (`results/campaign_benchmark.json`), trust-region conformal policies maintain `0.000` OOD picks and reach `0.10` simple regret on Doyle Buchwald-Hartwig (`0.00` on Shields Direct Arylation).
- **History**: Initialized in `41dae77` (2026-11-18: ESOL conformal demo) -> `6957f12` (Phase-2 Lean 4 & wire) -> `e37de97` (PR #31: 11-dataset registry) -> `f72af29` (PR #36: balanced group-packing splits, HTE `onehot_morgan` + `ensemble_mahalanobis` + `cqr_r` across 11 real CSVs, 10-seed sequential BO campaigns, frozen ESOL `autolab_wire`, and 23-theorem Lean CI).

  - Frozen SHA-pinned ESOL `ConformalTrustModel` in `autolab_wire.py` (`n_calib = 169`, `thr = 0.6750`), scoring RDKit SMILES directly and expanding `tau` strictly on low-residual `(descriptors, measured_y)` ledger entries.

- **Object**: Verifies whether candidate molecules proposed by L6/L5 scientific planners (lab-ask-why, lab-decide) are directly purchasable from chemical vendors or makeable in N steps from purchasable building blocks, computing conservative leaf-only stoichiometric cost lower bounds strictly from real listed catalog prices while keeping missing prices null and unverified molecules unknown.

  - Machine-checked `BuyOrMakeLean` Lean 4.36.0 theorems (`unpriced_never_passes_budget`, `routeStoichiometryOK`) compiled and audited in CI across every retrosynthetic template.
