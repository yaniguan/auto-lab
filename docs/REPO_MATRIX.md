## `L6` · [lab-ask-why](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-ask-why) (`7f945fa`)

- **Object**: Epistemic disagreement and rationale-probing module (`L6`) for the Auto-Lab brain: rather than collapsing scientific uncertainty into a binary verdict, it retrieves verbatim evidence sentences on each side of a claim, surfaces necessary assumptions, and frames the discriminating experimental result that would resolve the disagreement.
