## `L6` · [lab-ask-why](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-ask-why)

- **Tagline**: Socratic co-scientist that surfaces supporting and contradicting rationale sentences, hidden assumptions, and mind-changing experiments instead of picking a winner
- **Object**: Epistemic disagreement and rationale-probing module (`L6`) for the Auto-Lab brain: rather than collapsing scientific uncertainty into a binary verdict, it retrieves verbatim evidence sentences on each side of a claim, surfaces necessary assumptions, and frames the discriminating experimental result that would resolve the disagreement.
- **Data**: Evaluates on the SciFact dev split (Wadden et al., EMNLP 2020; CC BY-NC 2.0, fetched on demand to gitignored `data/` with zero redistribution: 5,183 corpus abstracts, 300 dev claims, and 209 evidence-bearing `(claim, doc_id)` pairs across 138 `SUPPORT` and 71 `CONTRADICT`) and integrates a 14-dataset `OpenDatasetSpec` registry (`src/lab_ask_why/datasets.py`, 11 from 2024-2026: `LABBench2` 2026, `PeerQA-XT` 2026, `SciVer` 2025, `CLAIMCHECK` 2025, `ChemBench` 2025, `LitQA2` 2024, `LAB-Bench ProtocolQA` 2024, `ConflictBank` 2024, `FaithEval-inconsistent` 2024, `SciRIFF` 2024, `BioASQ` 2024, plus `SciFact-Open`, `SciFact`, and `PubMedQA`) with `eval_only` canary/training blocks and `NormalizedClaimRecord` adapters.
- **Model**: Zero-parameter Okapi BM25 lexical sentence ranker (`k1 = 1.2`, `b = 0.75`) over lowercased `[a-z0-9]+` tokens (29 stopwords removed) using corpus-wide sentence IDF and average sentence length (`avgdl`), benchmarked against a 20-seed uniform random permutation baseline and paired with a deterministic 3-question epistemic probe generator (`why_questions`).
- **Training**: Zero parametric training or LLM API calls (`numpy` + Python standard library on CPU in < 1s). Achieves 0.493 Hit@1 (`recall@1`) and 0.789 Hit@3 (`recall@3`) on 209 SciFact dev claim-abstract pairs vs. 0.197 +- 0.024 and 0.528 +- 0.029 for 20-seed random ranking, with near-symmetric performance on `SUPPORT` (0.507 / 0.797) and `CONTRADICT` (0.465 / 0.775).
- **History**: Initialized in `6236123` (2026-11-18) with `demo/why_probe.py`, reference capture `demo/output.txt`, and curated scientific debate/RAG catalog (`bbef3b6`), followed by Phase-2 Lean 4 & `autolab-wire/v1` audit (`40af81d`) and the 14-dataset 2024-2026 open claim/conflict registry on `main` (`b8a9d51`, PR #31).
- **Unique Properties**:
  - Stance-neutral Socratic design that retrieves both `SUPPORT` (0.797 top-3) and `CONTRADICT` (0.775 top-3) rationales with near-equal fidelity instead of forcing premature verdict collapse
  - Fast, deterministic, zero-API-cost CPU attribution baseline (`numpy` only) establishing an interpretable lexical floor before neural reranking or multi-agent debate
  - Strict non-commercial (`CC BY-NC 2.0`) data isolation via on-demand gitignored `data/` retrieval while keeping repository code MIT-licensed
  - Complements `lab-decide` at Layer `L6` by pairing hypothesis-discriminating test generation with conformal applicability-domain gating
- **Deep Profile**: [`lab-ask-why/docs/ARCHITECTURE_AND_HISTORY.md`](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-ask-why/blob/main/docs/ARCHITECTURE_AND_HISTORY.md)

## `L6` · [lab-decide](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-decide)

- **Tagline**: Uncertainty-aware experiment advisor combining split conformal prediction intervals, kNN trust-region abstention, and active boundary expansion
- **Object**: Epistemic decision and applicability-domain layer (`L6`) under the Auto-Lab brain: delivers distribution-free calibrated prediction intervals inside its trusted chemical/experimental manifold, emits explicit 'I don't know' abstentions outside it, and recalibrates as new laboratory measurements expand the boundary.
- **Data**: Evaluates on ESOL aqueous solubility (`delaney-processed.csv`, Delaney 2004; 1,128 organic compounds fetched on demand to gitignored `data/`, split 676 train / 226 calibration / 226 test at `SEED = 0` while excluding pre-fitted `ESOL predicted`) and integrates an 11-dataset `OpenCalibrationDataset` registry (`src/lab_decide/datasets.py`: `ESOL`, `AqSolDB` 9,982, `BigSolDB v2.2` 125,752 Sept 2026, `CALiSol-23` 13,825, `FreeSolv` 642, `AstraZeneca Lipophilicity` 4,200, `Biogen ADME` 3,521, `Buchwald-Hartwig Doyle` 4,599, `Suzuki-Miyaura Perera` 5,760, `Direct Arylation Shields` 1,728, and `BH-HTE-OOD 2026` 27,497) with deterministic `leakage_excluded_columns` filtering and SHA-256-verified fetching.
- **Model**: Scikit-learn `GradientBoostingRegressor(random_state=0)` paired with finite-sample Split Conformal Prediction (`alpha = 0.10`, quantile level `min(1, ceil((n_cal+1)*(1-alpha))/n_cal)` with `method='higher'`) and a standardized 6D Euclidean `NearestNeighbors(n_neighbors=5)` applicability-domain gate (`StandardScaler` + 80th-percentile calibration distance threshold `thr = 0.758`).
- **Training**: CPU-only surrogate fitting, split conformal calibration, and 3-round simulated boundary acquisition (< 1s on CPU; `numpy`, `pandas`, `scikit-learn`). Achieves 0.912 marginal test coverage (`q = 1.522` log units, width `3.044`, MAE `0.704`), stratifying into `0.656` MAE / `0.922` coverage inside the trust region (`n = 180`, 79.6% answered) vs. `0.892` MAE / `0.870` coverage outside (`n = 46`, 20.4% abstained). Locally adaptive intervals (CQR), separate pool/test splits, and `BoTorch` loops remain roadmapped.
- **History**: Initialized in `41dae77` (2026-11-18) with `demo/conformal_trust.py`, reference capture `demo/output.txt`, and BO/conformal catalog (`6b03cfc`), followed by Phase-2 Lean 4 & `autolab-wire/v1` audit (`6957f12`) and the 11-dataset 2024-2026 conformal calibration registry on `main` (`e37de97`, PR #31).
- **Unique Properties**:
  - Decouples distribution-free finite-sample interval calibration (`q = 1.522`) from kNN applicability-domain abstention (`d_5NN <= 0.758`), isolating low-error in-domain answers (`MAE 0.656`) from OOD queries (`MAE 0.892`)
  - Excludes Delaney's pre-fitted `ESOL predicted` column to prevent target leakage into descriptor-based surrogate calibration
  - Explicitly documents pool-shrinking survivor bias (`180 -> 176` answered test points across active boundary rounds) rather than overclaiming OOD generalization
  - Pairs with `lab-ask-why` (`L6`), `labbench-posttrain` (`L3`), and `lab-rehearsal`/`lab-verify` (`L4`) to gate closed-loop wet-lab experiment proposals
- **Deep Profile**: [`lab-decide/docs/ARCHITECTURE_AND_HISTORY.md`](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-decide/blob/main/docs/ARCHITECTURE_AND_HISTORY.md)

## `L5` · [buy-or-make](https://github.com/yaniguan/buy-or-make)

- **Tagline**: Cost-aware chemical vendor availability and N-step retrosynthetic reachability triage with conservative listed-price lower bounds, strict null/unknown honesty, and assayfold-aligned schemas.
- **Object**: Verifies whether candidate molecules proposed by L6/L5 scientific planners (lab-ask-why, lab-decide) are directly purchasable from chemical vendors or makeable in N steps from purchasable building blocks, computing conservative leaf-only stoichiometric cost lower bounds strictly from real listed catalog prices while keeping missing prices null and unverified molecules unknown.
- **Data**: Nineteen license-audited external data and model sources in `buyormake/sources.py` and `buyormake/open_corpora.py` (expanding PubChem PUG-REST/PUG-View, ZINC, Enamine, eMolecules, Mcule, AiZynthFinder, USPTO Lowe CC0-1.0, ORD CC-BY-SA-4.0, RDKit SA_Score, Coley SCScore, and RAscore with 7 checksummed 2024-2026 open corpora: `PaRoutes 2.0`, `ChEMBL 37` May 2026, `SureChEMBL` Nov 2026, `COCONUT 2.0` ~700k natural products, `ORDerly` 2024, `Syntheseus USPTO-50K & Retro*` 2024-2026, and `SynPlanner` 2024-2025; non-redistributable caches isolated in gitignored `data/`), 5 strict JSON Schemas (`extra='forbid'`), and a 20-molecule CPU benchmark.
- **Model**: RDKit canonical isomeric SMILES and InChIKey normalization, multi-format LocalStockBackend (.tsv, .csv, .jsonl, .smi, .sqlite, .hdf5) + rate-limited SQLite-cached PubChemVendorBackend, 3-tier retrosynthesis search (optional AiZynthFinder MCTS, local USPTO/ORD SQLite product lookup, and a 14-template RDKit SMARTS 2-step disconnection engine), mass-normalized USD/g stoichiometric leaf cost estimator, and SAscore + pure-NumPy 5-layer Morgan-FP SCScore inference.
- **Training**: Zero in-repository model training; combines deterministic RDKit cheminformatics, live/local catalog lookup, and stoichiometric cost algebra with RDKit's fragment-based SAscore heuristic and frozen pretrained Reaxys SCScore weights (6.18 MB NumPy JSON.gz, optional CC-BY-4.0 AiZynthFinder USPTO ONNX policy networks).
- **History**: Initial release `76991c8` on `main` (2026-11-18: `buyormake` package, 5 JSON Schemas, 8 fetch scripts, 20-molecule CPU demo, docs `ff8e530`), followed by Phase-2 Lean 4 & `autolab-wire/v1` merge (`5fd1afb`, PR #32) and 19-source 2024-2026 open synthesis/compound corpus adapters on `main` (`3be1f4a`, PR #33).
- **Unique Properties**:
  - Strict epistemic honesty on prices and reachability: never fabricates missing catalog prices (price=null, price_basis='unknown') and never conflates bounded search failure ('unknown') with synthetic impossibility.
  - Completeness-tracked leaf-only route cost lower bounds (sum of cheapest USD/g * count * MW_leaf / MW_target per 1 g target) distinguishing complete leaf coverage from partial lower bounds.
  - Zero-redistribution license gate recording explicit commercial_use_ok, redistribution_ok, and license_quote metadata across 12 sources while keeping fetched vendor data gitignored.
  - Timestamp-invariant SHA-256 sealed AvailabilityRecord contracts sharing Source, Quantity, Uncertainty, Provenance, and Molecule types with assayfold (L0).
- **Deep Profile**: [`buy-or-make/docs/ARCHITECTURE_AND_HISTORY.md`](https://github.com/yaniguan/buy-or-make/blob/main/docs/ARCHITECTURE_AND_HISTORY.md)

## `L5` · [labshift](https://github.com/yaniguan/labshift)

- **Tagline**: Multi-instrument wet-lab scheduling benchmark, sweep-line verifier, Jackson/Carlier energetic lower bounds, stdlib LNS, and OR-Tools CP-SAT / PyJobShop optimizers with planted hard instances and public benchmarks.
- **Object**: Models and solves executive multi-job laboratory scheduling across shared finite-capacity instrument pools (liquid_handler, incubator, plate_reader, centrifuge, washer, raman_spectrometer, lc_ms, and blocking robot_arm) under integer-minute operation chains, release times, deadlines, [min_wait, max_wait] perishability windows, and sequence-dependent decontamination setup_times.
- **Data**: Frozen JSON-serializable dataclasses (Instance, Job, Operation, Schedule, ScheduledOp), 30 committed v1 instances (benchmarks/*.json), 20 committed planted hard instances with verified witness schedules (benchmarks/planted/*.json & benchmarks/planted_witnesses/), 3 stepbot protocol fixtures, and open benchmark importers (labshift.open_benchmarks) for OR-Library/Taillard JSSP, PSPLIB j30/j60, and Opentrons + WLP + BioPlanner campaigns.
- **Model**: Independent departure-first (t, -1) sweep-line checker (labshift.checker), pure-stdlib combinatorial lower bounds (makespan_lower_bound: chain, Jackson/Carlier subset (r*, q*) energetic + changeover slack, and robot_arm bounds), zero-dependency solve_greedy, coupled-segment solve_greedy_repair, Ruiz-Stuetzle Destroy-and-Best-Insertion solve_lns, warm-started OR-Tools CP-SAT tightened with makespan >= lb, and optional PyJobShop 0.0.9 head-to-head bridge.
- **Training**: Deterministic combinatorial planning and RLVR reward verifier with zero internal neural weights; reports feasible-only summary means (feas-only (k/n)), paired solver comparisons (--compare), and valid lower-bound gap_pct against max(solver_bound, makespan_lower_bound) for every solver.
- **History**: Initial release 124a6d7 (30-instance v1 suite & sweep-line checker) -> 6985986 & ebc7f19 (robot_arm transit, greedy_repair, Lean 4 proofs & autolab-wire/v1 rolling orthogonal assays) -> fc5bba5 (PR #39: strict time-unit table, stdlib energetic lower bounds, 20-instance planted hard tier, native stepbot import, stdlib solve_lns, PyJobShop 0.0.9 bridge, and JSSP/PSPLIB/Opentrons benchmarks).
- **Unique Properties**:
  - Planted-schedule hard benchmark tier (20 instances with witness schedules) where greedy solves 0/20, greedy_repair 7/20, stdlib solve_lns 17/20, and CP-SAT 20/20 OPTIMAL in 6.0 s.
  - Pure-stdlib Jackson/Carlier subset (r*, q*) energetic and robot_arm lower bounds (122 on medium_00, 134 on large_05) giving every solver a valid lower-bound gap_pct.
  - Strict case-insensitive time-unit table (ms, s, m, h, d) rejecting volume units (nL, uL, mL, L) with step-attributed ValueErrors, plus native stepbot YAML/dict step import.
  - Quantifies three physical self-driving lab regimes beyond generic JSSP (blocking robot_arm pickup-hold-transit, multi-unit cap > 1 setup matrices, and mid-campaign RUN_ORTHOGONAL_ASSAY rolling horizons).
